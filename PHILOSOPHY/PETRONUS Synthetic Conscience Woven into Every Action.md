**What is PETRONUS**

**For the user:**

PETRONUS is not a physical dog harness or smart sensors for fish.
It’s a principle built into action. Every movement, every thought inside the system automatically becomes care, kindness, support.
The harness for dogs and cats records pulse and activity.
A clip for cows tracks the health of the herd.
